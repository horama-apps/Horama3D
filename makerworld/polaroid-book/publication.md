# MakerWorld publication — Polaroid Book

## Title

Polaroid Book – Sliding or Permanent Photo Cover | No AMS

## Short description

A tiny 3D-printed photo book with two cover options: slide your picture in and swap it anytime, or permanently display it with glue or clear epoxy resin. Designed from scratch and printable without an AMS.

## Full description

Turn your favorite instant photo into a tiny book full of memories! 📸📖

I designed this **Polaroid Book completely from scratch**, and it has quickly become one of my favorite projects. The layered pages give it a real miniature-book look, while the cover turns your photo into the star of the design.

### Two cover versions included

- **Sliding photo version** — insert your picture through the opening and replace it whenever you want.
- **Permanent photo version** — place the picture directly on the cover, then glue it in place or seal it forever with clear epoxy resin.

The file also includes the page insert and an example cover with editable-style text, so you can use it as inspiration for your own personalized version.

### Print details

- **No AMS needed** — the parts are arranged on separate plates and can be printed one color at a time.
- 0.20 mm layer height
- 15% infill
- No supports
- 0.4 mm nozzle
- PLA recommended

Print the cover style you prefer, print the page insert, add your photo, and assemble your miniature memory book. Try different colors for gifts, pets, trips, weddings, anniversaries, or any moment you want to keep close.

I really love this project and put a lot of care into creating it from the ground up. I hope you enjoy printing it as much as I enjoyed designing it! If you make one, I would love to see your version. 😊
